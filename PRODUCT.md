# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Professional financial analysts, auditors, backoffice operators, and compliance managers in Brazil who need to research instruments, market prices, historical quotations, indices, and portfolio compositions, and track the synchronization and provenance of official public B3 data.

## Product Purpose

Provide a highly reliable, lucid, and efficient set of REST APIs and a read-only analytical MCP server based exclusively on official public B3 data, with normalized records, explicit source provenance, and auditable synchronization.

## Design Principles

- Follow OpenAPI best practices with explicit, typed, source-specific contracts.
- Treat security as a first-class concern.
- Reuse proven solutions and shared domain services.
- Preserve official B3 provenance and distinguish each source family.
- Make synchronization idempotent, deterministic, observable, and auditable.
- Preserve financial precision and temporal instrument identity.
- Clearly communicate data limitations, including unadjusted historical prices.
- Keep infrastructure simple and resource use efficient.
